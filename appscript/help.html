💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

🎨 Creating Great Prompts

Start Broad, Then Refine: Begin with general elements (character type, environment) then add specific details.
Balance is Key: Don't overload with too many conflicting styles or elements. 3-5 well-chosen options work best.
Experiment with Combinations: Try unexpected pairings like "Victorian cyberpunk" or "watercolor photorealism" for unique results.
Use the Multi-Format Feature: Different AI generators respond better to different prompt styles. Try multiple formats!
Save Your Winners: When you create a great prompt, export it to build a library of successful combinations.

🚀 Workflow Optimization

Use Templates: Create base combinations for different project types (portraits, landscapes, action scenes) as starting points.
Organize Your Imports: When importing AI prompts, add descriptive notes to help categorize them properly.
Clear Selections Strategically: Use section-specific clear buttons to modify just one aspect without starting over.
Test Variations: Change one element at a time to understand how each category affects the final image.
Keep Notes: Use the Log sheet to track which combinations worked best for different AI generators.

🎯 Technical Tips

Camera Settings Matter: Technical details like lens type and depth of field can dramatically change results.
Lighting Sets Mood: The right lighting can transform the entire feel of your image. Experiment!
Composition Rules: Using established photography principles (rule of thirds, golden ratio) often yields better results.
Style Consistency: Match your art style choice across character, scene, and camera for cohesive results.
Reference Photos: Keep a collection of reference images that match your desired style for comparison.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Dropdowns Empty? Run the diagnostics tool from the menu to refresh all categories.
Import Not Working? Ensure your RAW_AI_DATA sheet follows the correct format with proper headers.
Slow Performance? Clear your browser cache and refresh the dashboard. Large imports can temporarily slow things down.
Export Issues? Check your Google Drive permissions and ensure you have sufficient storage space.
Need Help? Use the debug tools in the menu to identify specific issues with data loading.
